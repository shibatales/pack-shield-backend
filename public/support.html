Help with Pack Shield

For support, bug reports, privacy questions, or App Store review questions, email support@packshield.app.

Bug Reports

Helpful bug reports include what you tapped, what you expected to happen, what actually happened, your iPhone or iPad model, and your iOS version. Screenshots are useful when they do not include sensitive information.

Purchases

Optional Shibit suit suites are purchased through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Use Restore Purchases in Pack Shield settings to ask the App Store to restore eligible purchases.

Text Messages

Text-message setup may be temporarily unavailable while carrier approval is pending. You can still save support people in the app and use phone calls.

Safety

Pack Shield is not medical advice, therapy, treatment, emergency response, or crisis care. If you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.
