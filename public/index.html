Pack Shield SMS Support

Pack Shield uses SMS to verify a user's phone number, invite a trusted sponsor, let the sponsor confirm their role, and help the sponsor set or rotate a private approval PIN.

Sponsors opt in by texting VERIFY followed by the invite code they receive from Pack Shield. Message frequency varies based on user and sponsor actions. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.
